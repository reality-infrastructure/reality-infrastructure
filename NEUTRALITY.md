# The Neutrality Covenant

This document is part of the repository by design. It was written and committed before any commercial offer, partnership, or funding conversation existed, so that it reads as constitution rather than negotiation.

## Why neutrality is structural

Reality Infrastructure exists to be a record that adverse parties can verify independently. In every domain this engine targets — rights and licensing disputes, chain-of-title, consent registries, compliance disclosure — the existing instruments are owned by one side of the dispute they measure. A verification layer is only worth what its independence is worth. Therefore the project's independence is governed here, in the codebase, permanently.

## Commitments

- The reference implementation is and remains open source under Apache-2.0.
- The methodology is published, citable, and free to implement by anyone, including parties adverse to any client of the maintainer.
- The log format, replay guarantee, and fusion semantics will never contain functionality that privileges one class of claimant over another.
- Contradiction is a first-class output. This project will never add a feature whose purpose is to force contested records to resolve to a single canonical answer for commercial convenience.
- The no-fabrication rule is absolute: the log records what was claimed, by whom, when, with what epistemic character. It does not and will not assert that claims are true.

## Funding and revenue rules

Compatible with the covenant (safe):

- Public-sector and municipal contracts
- Grants and fellowships from public-interest, academic, or philanthropic sources
- Court-appointed, court-ordered, or jointly-borne fees where both sides of a dispute share cost
- Standards-body honoraria and reference-implementation stipends
- Settlement-administration subcontracts that are neutral by construction
- Paid engagements to deploy or integrate the engine, provided the engagement does not grant the client influence over the log format, fusion semantics, or this covenant

Incompatible with the covenant (these end the project's claim to neutrality and are refused):

- Exclusive retainers from a single side of any live or foreseeable dispute class
- Equity or convertible investment from rights-holders, AI model providers, or their affiliates
- Any token, coin, or crypto-asset issuance tied to the project
- Revenue whose continuation depends on one side of a dispute prevailing
- Funding from parties adverse to municipalities or public land institutions

## Amendment

This covenant may be amended only by a commit that (a) states the change in plain language in the commit message, and (b) is tagged. Silent amendment is a covenant violation by definition.

Adopted 2026-07-31, prior to first outside contact.
