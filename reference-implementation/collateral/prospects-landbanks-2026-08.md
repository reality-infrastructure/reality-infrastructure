## Prioritization notes (from the data, not opinion)
- **Michigan first**: the MALB directory publishes named contacts + emails for ~48
  members — lowest-friction, and Genesee/Detroit have the largest inventories
  (Detroit ~63.5k properties; a Reality Audit scales with inventory).
- **Ohio second**: 71 county land banks, most running on county records identical in
  shape to the audit's inputs; Ohio county auditor data is public and structured.
- **NY third**: BENLIC's ED transition (Aug 21) and Syracuse's 2,378-property
  inventory are concrete openings.
- The verify-before-send (v) rows must be confirmed on the org's own site first.
