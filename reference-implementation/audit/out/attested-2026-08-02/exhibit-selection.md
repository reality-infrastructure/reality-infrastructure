## Why the honest number is stronger than three

The two failure causes are exactly the audit's disciplines doing their job:

1. **(c) failures (16 parcels)** trace to one unattested separator-variant
   string, `SO SUB LAND/BK/DEV`, that escaped the pre-registered near-miss net
   — a classifier finding (F1), reported, not fixed mid-contract. Until the
   operator attests that string, a CONTRADICTED verdict whose chain contains it
   rests on an unattested identity assumption; such a parcel cannot be a
   client-facing exhibit under criterion (c), whatever it looks like.
2. **(b) failures (23 parcels)** trace to the transfer-declaration dataset
   recording conveyances with blank grantor/grantee — the pre-registered
   coverage caveat made concrete. The contradiction may well be real, but the
   citation chain cannot show both parties verbatim, so the exhibit bar is not
   met from this dataset alone. (Recorder-of-Deeds images would cure this; new
   data is a different contract.)

Exhibit 1 (`exhibit-1-dolton-29-02-408-053-CLIENT-DO-NOT-SEND-PROSPECTS.md`)
survives everything, and is additionally the pre-registered known-answer,
independently corroborated by the M-RI-11 dossier.
